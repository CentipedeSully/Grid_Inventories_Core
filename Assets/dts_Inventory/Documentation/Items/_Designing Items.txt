Designing Items


Here's how to design and create new items from start to finish.

1) Create an new itemData (ScriptableObject) asset in your project files that'll 
   represent the new item template. This asset defines and represents all 
   instances of the item. Changing an itemData changes all instances of the item,
   and all created items refer back to their itemData for their data.
   [Right-Click -> Create -> Item Data] in your project folder.

2) Fill out the newly-created itemData. To see what each field of the ItemData represents,
   visit the "_ArchitectureOfItemData" document. It covers each property and its use in detail.

3) Draw the item's sprite:
   The default cell size for the prefab Grid is 32x32 pixels.
   That can be easily changed in the grid object's grid component.
   If you want an object's sprite to span multiple cells and be visually consistent with your
   previously-defined spacial definition, then you'll need to resize your canvas to match the item's 
   pixel size from the start. Meaning you'll first define your item's spacial definition, then you'll 
   identify your desired cellSize in your grids. From there, you'll then calculate that item's canvas size.
   Here's examples of how to do that.

   Ex: The default 'Cube' item has a (1x1) spacial definition. The specified cellsize is 32x32 pixels (defined in the grid).
       So, the cube's canvas size needs to be 32x32 pixels (or any ratio that's 1x1, since its single-celled). 
       
       Boards are defined with a (4x1) spacial definition, so THEIR canvas needs to also be at a ratio of 4x1 [128x32 pixels 
       if we're going the pixel perfect route].
       
       "Things" (the weird, tall statue-like objects) are (1x2) in their spacial definition, so their canvases are at a ratio
       of 1x2 (32x64 pixels).
       
       The Hollow is a large (3x3) item. Can you infer that item's canvas ratio? 
       

   Here's your exact item's SpriteSize formula:
       - canvas dimensions = cellWidth * cellSize , cellHeight * cellSize  [where cellSize is your grid's defined cell size]

4) Once your sprite is drawn and added to the ItemData, your item is completed. To test it, simply add it to your ItemCreator's
   'ItemsList' list and enter playmode.

   The InvGrid comes with debug methods to add and remove items. Ensure your itemdata is set in the 'Param Item Data' debug property,
   and then click the "Add Item" box. 

   Your item should appear within the grid (if there's room for it to fit).
   
   From there, you may click on the item to pick it up and rClick to place it back down, and use Q and E to rotate the item (when picked up).
   Debug moving it around as you see fit. (assuming you haven't changed the scene's default input settings [also assuming the input method
   is compatible with your project if your importing this package into a preexisting project])

   Congratulations. You've created your first custom item!
