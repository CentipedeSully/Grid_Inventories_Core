Overlaying Custom Images


Generally speaking, if you want to add extra effects to your grids, you may.
Just create your own container within the grid and be sure that container is
included within the InvGrid's 'OtherContainers' list. The grid will be responsible 
for resizing and maintaing your container after that.

Then, if you want to apply graphics to specific cells, you can call the InvGrid's
'OverlayRectTranformOntoGrid' method. It takes a graphic, your target container,
the cell where you want your graphic to be centered, and whether or no you want the grapic
to be scaled to the cell's size.

Keep in mind, you'll be responsible for maintaining any graphics you choose to add. The grid
won't be responsible for remembering your custom graphic(s)'s placements, so you'll need to do
that yourself.
