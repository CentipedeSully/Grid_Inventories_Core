Rotating Items


When an InvItem gets rotated, it transforms both its entire spatial definition AND its own gameobject's rotation
to fit the requested rotation state.

Here are the default rotation states:

*****
public enum ItemRotation
{
    None, //0 or 360
    Once, //90
    Twice, //180
    Thrice, //270
}



public enum RotationDirection
{
    Clockwise,
    CounterClockwise
}
*****

These enums are used to tell InvItems how to rotate themselves.


Rotations are calculated in the ItemData class by a couple statis utilities,
and generally speaking, you shouldn't have a need to mess with them.


To rotate an item, simply call the InvItem's 'RotateItem (RotationDirection direction)' method,
and the rotation and transformations will happen by themselves.
